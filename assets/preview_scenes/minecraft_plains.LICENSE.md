The `minecraft_plains` scene's skybox (the six `minecraft_plains_*.jpg` faces) comes from https://github.com/JannisX11/blockbench-scenes (`minecraft/plains`), re-encoded as JPEG. The scene's ground blocks and tall grass are left out (they stood in the way of the model, in the preview and in the editor), and so is its field of view (the camera keeps the viewer's own).
